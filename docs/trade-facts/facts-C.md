### [WS-SILICA] https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/dust/silica-dust-in-the-workplace/
1. "Silicosis is a progressive and deadly disease that causes fibrosis of the lungs from the inhalation of respirable crystalline silica (RCS) dust."
2. "As a PCBU, you have a duty to eliminate, or use controls to minimise worker exposure to the hazard of, and risks from, RCS dust."
3. "Silica is a natural substance found in concrete, bricks, rocks, stone (including artificial or engineered stone found in composite kitchen benchtops), sand and clay. RCS dust is created when materials containing silica are cut, ground, drilled, sanded, polished or otherwise disturbed."
4. "construction: concrete, stone, bricks, mortar, fibre cement products"
5. "Engineered stone has approximately 90% silica compared with natural stone like marble and limestone which have around 2% silica."
6. "Keep the work material (eg concrete, engineered stone benchtops) wet while work is carried out on them (eg cutting, polishing, etc). Use on-tool water suppression systems to keep dust out of the air"
7. "For example, tools used for cutting, grinding or polishing concrete and masonry should provide water to the blade and/or be fitted with an on-tool extraction system."
8. "Use an H-class HEPA-filtered vacuum cleaner in accordance with Standard AS60335-2-69. This includes when working at someone's home (eg to fit a bench). Workers should not use the household vacuum cleaner to remove dust."
9. "Set up exclusion zones with signs to mark the boundaries of work areas where RCS dust is created."
10. "PPE is the least effective control measure. It should not be the first or only control measure you consider." / "Carry out fit testing for each worker who will wear a respirator that requires a seal against the face."
11. "As a PCBU you must, so far as is reasonably practicable, monitor workplace conditions if exposure to a particular health risk warrants it."
