## S2. PGDB Self-certification Notice 2026 (curl, PDF)

- "An approved self-certifying practitioner can certify work that: a. meets the definitions of self-certifiable plumbing or self-certifiable drainlaying, and b. is the subject of a building consent that allows the work to be self-certified."

- "Approved self-certifying practitioners must: ... b. attend the site and inspect work in-person in order to certify it ... d. issue a certificate of compliance in the form required by the Board for all work they certify"

## S4. PGDB: Advice for consumers (curl)
https://www.pgdb.co.nz/advice_for_consumers/
- Plumbing not DIY: "Undertake any work involved in fixing or unfixing any pipe, plumbing fixture or appliance including; any trap, waste or soil pipe, ventilation pipe, or overflow pipe and any pipe that supplies or is intended to supply water."
- "A certifying plumber is responsible for the testing, verification and the supervision of plumbers, restricted plumbers, limited certificate (trainee plumbers) and exempted persons."
- Sanitary plumbing / drainlaying and BCA: "Must comply with the building code and where building consent is required; the work must be checked by the building inspector from the building control authority in your area (your local council)." / "All drainlaying work must comply with the Building Code and where a building consent is required, the work must be checked by the building inspector..."
- Gasfitting not DIY: "Undertake any work involved in installing, altering or repairing gas appliances including: Fixing or unfixing of any gas pipes, ventilation or flue pipes / Commissioning gas appliances and ensuring their safe operation (including the testing, setting, checking and adjusting of safety devices, combustion conditions and controls associated with the gas installation and ensuring there is adequate ventilation) / Work on appliances fitted to ships, boats, caravans, motorhomes, trains and any other vehicles..."
- "A certifying gasfitter is responsible for the testing, verification and the supervision of gasfitters, restricted gasfitters, limited certificate (trainee gasfitters) and exempted persons."
- "A certifying gasfitter must provide a signed gasfitting certificate where they have done gasfitting work at the consumer's property."
- "However, if they have not done the work themselves, the certifying gasfitter that provides the certificate must be satisfied that the work was carried out in accordance with the law with and compliance documents that specify how gasfitting work must be done."
- "The gasfitter is required by law to provide a gasfitting certificate within 20 working days of the gasfitting work being connected to the gas supply."
- "The gasfitter must also keep a copy of any gasfitting certificate they issue for seven years."
- "They may be needed if you sell the property or for insurance purposes."
- "Gasfitting certificates are a legal document required under the Gas (Safety and Measurement) Regulations 2010." / "Under the Building Act 2004, they are recognised as an Energy Works Certificate."
- Drainlaying not DIY: "Undertake any work involving the installation, alteration or repairs to a drain, including the fixing or unfixing of a drain to an onsite wastewater system (septic tank) or any trap."
- Drain definition: "Drains are underground pipes which carry foul or surface water (stormwater) and enter either an onsite waste water disposal system (septic tank) or a sewer which is likely to be controlled by a territorial authority (district council)."
- "Drainlaying work however does not include pipes in the control of the Crown or a local authority. Drainlaying also does not include an open jointed or perforated drain for collecting and removing ground water."
- "A certifying drainlayer is responsible for the testing, verification and the supervision of drainlayers, restricted drainlayers, limited certificate (trainee drainlayers) and exempted persons."

## S5. PGDB: Disciplinary hearings (curl)
https://www.pgdb.co.nz/complaints_and_disciplinary_processes/disciplinary_processes/
- "Plumbers, gasfitters, and drainlayers are regulated because of the risk that sanitary plumbing, gasfitting and drainlaying poses to public health and safety."
- Offences (s 89): "carrying out negligent or incompetent work or causing it to be carried out / carrying out work that does not comply with the law ... / doing work they are not authorised to do / ... failing to provide a return required by law (e.g. a gas certificate) or providing a false or misleading return"
- Penalties (s 106): "cancel the registered person's registration, or licence, or both ... suspend the registered person's practising licence ... order the registered person to pay a fine of not more than $10,000"
- "under section 107 of the Act the Board can also order the registered person to pay a contribution towards the costs of the investigation, prosecution and hearing."

## S10. NZI Liability Broadform wording NZ5060/10 03/18 (WebFetch, PDF; curl 403)
https://www.nzi.co.nz/content/dam/insurance-brands-nz/nzi/nz/en/documents/nzi/liability/nzi-liability-broadform-pw-nz5060-10-0318.pdf
- 2.1: "You are insured for all sums that you become legally liable to pay arising from injury and/or damage that happens during the period of insurance, caused by an event in connection with the business."
- 5.8 Faulty workmanship: "You are not insured for liability for the cost of performing, completing, repairing, rectifying, correcting or improving any work undertaken by you or on your behalf." (exception for resultant damage to other property)
- 5.19(b): "property in your control or possession"; 4.1 optional extension covers "damage to property you are or have been working on"; 4.1 note: no cover "in connection with any defect in any design, plan or specification."
- 5.24: "You are not insured for liability in connection with any: underground cables, underground pipes, other underground facilities."
- 3.16: "inquired with the appropriate authorities or owners to verify the existence of the cables, pipes or other underground facilities" / "sighted a plan of their location if such a plan exists" / "utilised a competent third party to confirm their location"
- 5.11: "You are not insured for liability in connection with hot work carried out away from your own premises."
- 3.7: "A safe distance shall be no less than 10 (ten) metres from where the hot work is being carried out." / "hose reels or fire extinguishers of a type and capacity suitable for the combustible material and the premises shall be kept adjacent to the area of hot work and be available for immediate use" / "a thorough examination of the area will be made for any signs of combustion immediately after completing the hot work and again an hour after ceasing the hot work"
- 5.4: "You are not insured for liability in connection with any error or omission in: service or advice given by you"; 3.2 extension restores cover where advice is given without charge (tool: "waives this when advice is provided uncharged").
- 5.9: "You are not insured for liability for: any fine or penalty imposed on you (whether under contract or statute)"
